SMALL BY CHOICE

A few deliberate trade-offs.

Apron is for trusted groups. The server is authoritative; messages inside it are not end-to-end encrypted. Use TLS to protect the connection.

There’s no public federation, global social network, built-in spam defense, or sybil resistance. If those are your requirements, Apron may not be the right fit.
